Tesla Model S

The Tesla Model S is an influential, battery-electric, full-size luxury liftback produced from 2012 to 2026. Known for pioneering the modern electric vehicle market, it offers blistering acceleration—such as the 1,020-hp Plaid trim hitting 0-100 km/h in 2.1 seconds—coupled with a practical hatchback layout and over 400 miles of range, price is approximately $94,990 to $109,990

Tesla Model 3

Tesla Model 3 is a mid-size, all-electric fastback sedan introduced in 2017. It starts around $36,990, offers up to nearly 400 miles of range depending on the configuration, and features a minimalist interior controlled almost entirely through a central touchscreen.

Tesla Model X

Tesla Model X is an all-electric, mid-size luxury crossover SUV produced from 2015 until its discontinuation in 2016-2026. Known for its iconic falcon-wing rear doors and blazing-fast Plaid acceleration, it seats up to seven people and delivers a range of up to 340–379 miles depending on the variant,$99,990 for the standard All-Wheel Drive (AWD) model and goes up to $129,990 for the high-performance Model X Plaid. Prices change based on add-ons, seat choices, and paint options.
